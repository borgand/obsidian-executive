# Callouts

## Obsidian callout types

> [!note]
> The weekly sync moves to Wednesday from next month.

> [!abstract] Summary
> Revenue grew in every region except the north, where two large contracts ended.

> [!info]
> The office is closed on public holidays. Support stays on call.

> [!todo]
> Update the onboarding checklist with the new laptop policy.

> [!tip] Faster reviews
> Keep pull requests under 400 lines and link the related issue.

> [!success]
> The backup restore test completed in 14 minutes.

> [!question]- Who owns the billing service? (collapsed)
> The payments team owns it since the reorganisation in March.

> [!warning]
> The certificate on the staging host expires in nine days.

> [!failure]
> The nightly import failed because the source file was empty.

> [!danger]
> Do not run the cleanup script against production.

> [!bug]
> Exported PDFs drop the last row of long tables.

> [!example]
> A good commit message: `fix(export): include final table row`.

> [!quote] Grace Hopper
> The most dangerous phrase in the language is "we've always done it this way."

> [!tip]+ Foldable and open
> Callouts can also fold. This one starts expanded.
>
> > [!note] Nested callout
> > Callouts can be nested inside each other.

## GitHub alert forms

> [!NOTE]
> Highlights information that readers should take into account.

> [!TIP]
> Optional information to help a reader be more successful.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Crucial information necessary for readers to succeed.

> [!WARNING]
> Critical content demanding immediate attention.

> [!CAUTION]
> Negative potential consequences of an action.
